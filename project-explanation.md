# EduNova — Project Explanation

## Introduction

EduNova is a fictional online learning platform that provides learners with an organized and accessible way to discover online courses.

## Intended Users

The platform is designed for college students, beginners, job seekers, and working professionals who want to improve their knowledge and develop new skills.

## Design Choices

The design uses indigo and light purple as its primary colors to create a modern and professional appearance. White backgrounds and dark text improve readability, while the Inter typeface maintains consistency throughout the interface.

The hero section communicates the platform's purpose through a prominent heading, supporting description, educational illustration, and call-to-action buttons.

Feature cards explain the benefits of the platform, while course cards help users explore available learning opportunities. Testimonials and FAQs provide additional context, and the final call-to-action encourages visitors to begin their learning journey.

## Responsive Design

Three layouts were created for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens. The content arrangement changes according to screen width while preserving consistent branding, typography, and spacing.

## Design Techniques

Auto Layout, reusable components, shared color styles, and typography styles are used where implemented in the Figma design to maintain consistency and improve the design workflow.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates fundamental UI/UX principles, including visual hierarchy, responsive design, consistency, and component reuse.
